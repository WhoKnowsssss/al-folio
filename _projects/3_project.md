This is an Aerospace Engineering competition, where we build a wooden RC aircraft to perform some functionality (hold some payload and release a banner this year) and compete against other schools. 

I was leading an aerodynamics design team. Our aircraft has a wingspan of 3m and a max speed of 35m/s (80 mph!).
